*Draft assets and campaign instructions. None of these drafts have been published, emailed, or activated as advertisements.*

## Launch positioning
**Hero slogan — unchanged:** BALLS DEEP IN COMFORT.

**Supporting campaign line — unchanged:** COCKPIT IS COMING. KEEP YOUR PANTS ON.

**Members Club promise:** First notice when COCKPIT's first men's underwear drop opens for orders. Do not promise a confirmed date, exclusive discount or guaranteed stock unless the business approves it and can deliver.

**Audience:** Australians interested in premium men's underwear, clean design, lifestyle/fitness and witty brands. Initial focus on Australia; international expansion only when shipping, payment and returns are operational.

**Primary conversion:** Confirmed, consented Members Club email subscribers.

## Three creative pillars
1. **ROOSTER ALERT:** The recognisable illustrated rooster and original crowing audio. Keep original artwork. Sound on, sound off versions with captions.
2. **KEEP YOUR PANTS ON:** Anticipation, punchy cuts, cheeky humour and controlled reveal.
3. **BALLS DEEP IN COMFORT:** Aspirational comfort, construction and fit education — without unverified performance or fibre claims.

## Short-form scripts (original, ready for filming)
### Reel A — “The Rooster” (10–12 seconds)
- 0–2s: Black screen. Original rooster crow plays; on-screen caption: “DID YOU HEAR THAT?”
- 2–5s: Show the **original** COCKPIT rooster artwork.
- 5–8s: Hard cut to logo and “BALLS DEEP IN COMFORT.”
- 8–12s: “COCKPIT IS COMING. KEEP YOUR PANTS ON.” + “JOIN THE MEMBERS CLUB”.
- Caption: “The first drop is on its way. You've been warned. 🐓 Get first dibs — link in bio.”

### Reel B — “Keep Your Pants On” (8–10 seconds)
- Tight shots of real packaging, stitching, materials or approved mockups clearly identified if applicable.
- Intercut three title cards: “KEEP” / “YOUR” / “PANTS ON.”
- Finish on COCKPIT logo with link CTA.
- Caption: “Good things require some restraint. First drop coming. Join the Members Club.”

### Reel C — “Why Fit Matters” (15 seconds)
- Show three fit problems: rolling waistband, bunching fabric, constant adjustment (with original footage).
- Text: “YOU SHOULD NOT HAVE TO THINK ABOUT YOUR UNDIES ALL DAY.”
- End card: “COCKPIT / BALLS DEEP IN COMFORT” and “See our fit guide”.
- Avoid claiming any COCKPIT product cures the problems before product testing.

## Tracking plan
- Each organic platform should use a UTM link to the Members Club with `utm_source`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=first_drop`, `utm_content`.
- Future paid social should use `utm_medium=paid_social`. Google Ads should use its platform tracking as well as consistent UTMs where appropriate.
- Conversion quality requires a *verified subscriber*; current in-page first-party event queue is **not** a connected analytics platform.
- KPI hierarchy: qualified consented email leads > successful paid orders when store launches > vanity follower totals.
- Weekly measures: visits, source, signup form completion, accepted subscribers, CPM/CPC when advertising, revenue and contribution margin when orders open.

## Budget and controls
No ad spend authorised. No campaign published. No marketing email sent. No external account created or changed. Confirm first drop's date, stock, margins and ecommerce stack before customer-facing launch commitments. Set a budget only with owner's approval.
