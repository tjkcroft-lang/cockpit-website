PRIVACY & LAUNCH UPDATES

COCKPIT is preparing its first men's underwear release. This notice explains the information requested through the Members Club signup form and how that information is intended to be used.

Information requested

The launch signup form asks for your first name, email address, and an optional mobile number. It also requests consent to receive COCKPIT marketing and launch notifications.

Why it's requested

The purpose of the list is to contact interested subscribers about COCKPIT's first drop and brand updates. Providing a mobile number is optional. Mobile numbers are not an automatic opt-in to SMS marketing: separate SMS consent would be required before marketing text messages are sent.

Form processing

At present the website's form is configured to send submissions through a third-party form-processing service, FormSubmit, to the COCKPIT contact email configured for the site. The form service may process information needed to deliver your submission. Read FormSubmit's website for information about that service.

Questions or changes

For questions about your registration, or to request access, correction or deletion of signup information, contact hello@cockpitau.com. Commercial marketing emails must provide a working way to unsubscribe.

COCKPIT's first-drop ordering, full email marketing system, and customer account facilities have not yet launched. Further privacy information may be added as those services become available.
